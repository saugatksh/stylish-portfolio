Overview

What is DineX?

DineX is a complete, cloud-based restaurant management system designed for restaurants of all sizes — from a small café to a multi-floor dining establishment. It connects every part of your operation in a single seamless, real-time platform.

From the moment a customer sits down, to the kitchen preparing food, to the cash counter settling the bill — every handoff is automated, tracked, and visible to the right people at the right time. No paper slips, no miscommunication, no guesswork.

🛡
Admin
Full control — manages menu, staff, tables, finances, analytics, and settings. The restaurant's command center.
🧑‍🍽
Waiter Panel
Takes orders on any tablet or phone, browses the live menu by category, and dispatches rounds directly to the kitchen or bar with one tap.
🍳
Kitchen / Bar
Live order display board — receives orders in seconds, tracks preparation status, and signals when dishes are ready. Auto-refreshes every 8 seconds.
💰
Cash Counter
Generates itemized bills with 13% VAT, applies discounts (preset 5–50%), processes cash or QR payments, and optionally sends bills via WhatsApp.

Getting Started

Up & Running in Minutes

Contact DineX & Choose a Plan
Select Starter, Business, or Pro based on your restaurant size. Receive Admin credentials instantly.
Log In to the Admin Panel
Go to the Admin login page, enter your credentials, and land directly on the full Dashboard.
Add Tables & Build Your Menu
Configure floors/sections with seating capacity; add menu items by category or bulk-upload via CSV template.
Create Staff Accounts
Add Waiter, Kitchen, Cash Counter, and Admin accounts with a click. Each logs in with their own credentials.
Go Live!
Share credentials with your team. Waiter → main URL | Kitchen → /kitchen | Cash Counter → /cash-counter | Admin → /admin/login.
